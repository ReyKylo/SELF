# Tomorrow's Career Launch — Practical Playbook

Do not wait for a perfect CRM before acting. First goal is a repeatable **research → apply → approach → follow up** flow.

## 30-minute setup

- [ ] Save the latest CV link/filename in Career → Documents. Keep the actual file safe separately.
- [ ] Choose 10–15 priority VC/PE/M&A target firms from existing company records (verify websites).
- [ ] Check live Careers/LinkedIn postings at their original sources; do **not** trust historical source records as open roles.
- [ ] Save at least three relevant opportunities, with accurate URL and application deadline when known.
- [ ] Identify 3–5 professionals to approach, with actual LinkedIn URLs and specific reasons.
- [ ] Send personalized messages manually and record the connection/request date.
- [ ] Set a reasonable follow-up date for conversations you have started.
- [ ] Export a backup after the session.

## Short outreach drafts — customize before sending

**LinkedIn connection request**

Hi <Name>, I'm a final-year Finance & Banking student at Universitas Indonesia exploring venture capital and private equity. I came across your work at <Firm> and would love to connect and learn from your experience. Thank you!

**Post-connection inquiry**

Hi <Name>, thanks for connecting. I'm currently exploring investment internships and have been following <SpecificFirmOrInvestmentTheme>. I would really appreciate any advice on the skills or application routes worth prioritizing at <Firm>. Thanks for your time.

**Cold email**

Subject: Internship Interest — <Firm> | Finance Student, Universitas Indonesia

Dear <Name>,

I'm a final-year Finance & Banking student at Universitas Indonesia interested in investing and company building. I've been following <Firm> because of <SpecificReason>. I wanted to ask whether your team is currently considering interns or whether there is a recommended application process. I've attached my CV for reference and would be grateful for any direction. Thank you for your time.

Best regards,  
<Your Name>

**Follow-up**

Hi <Name>, just following up on my previous note regarding internship opportunities at <Firm>. I appreciate your busy schedule and would be grateful for any guidance whenever convenient. Thank you.

These drafts are intentionally generic starting points and must be personalized based on **verifiable** company/person context. They are **not** queued or auto-sent by Rey.
