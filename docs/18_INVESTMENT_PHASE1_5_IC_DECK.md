## User experience

### Part A: Defend your investment thesis
Six historically relevant investment committee rounds require a user-written initial answer, a second answer to an adversarial follow-up, and an attached Research Notebook evidence note:

1. **Deal pricing:** reconcile the public memo’s US$20m vs US$25m pre-money entries.
2. **Quality of growth:** customer additions vs churn and retention.
3. **Unit economics:** distinguish simplified CAC payback from the memo’s churn-adjusted measure.
4. **Competitive moat:** identify plausible threats and evidence that could disprove the thesis.
5. **Financial downside:** defend new-customer, churn, CAC and ARPU assumptions.
6. **Decision:** invest, pass or seek diligence, with defensible terms and reversal conditions.

An optional rubric offers prompts on what a rigorous answer must cover, not a model-generated answer or false green professional grade. “Prepared” indicates **coverage, a follow-up answer and a source note**, not that the argument is correct. Retrospective or outside-cutoff evidence is not eligible for as-of-2010 defense completeness. Learner responses can be downloaded as Markdown.

### Part B: Editable Deck Studio
- Import up to six starting **user-authored** memo sections as slides. No invented financial recommendations.
- Edit slide claim/title, eyebrow, body, native text-based metric and its historical/scenario label, speaker notes, source note and slide layout.
- Four layouts: thesis, split argument, evidence spotlight, numeric insight.
- Three restrained palettes: navy, paper and sage.
- Add, delete, select and reorder up to 16 slides.
- Preview at 16:9 aspect ratio. Shows clear source/assumption label; unsupported claims and out-of-date financial snapshots are flagged.
- Download structured deck source JSON, and **a native editable PowerPoint (.pptx)** containing real editable vector shapes and text boxes, built client-side with a minimal, dependency-free OOXML ZIP exporter. Every slide includes a source URL or explicit source-needed warning.
- No AI-generated investment recommendation, no flattened image-only PPTX, no silent publication of private work.

## Original historic cut-off and quality gates

- Original source: https://www.bvp.com/memos/shopify (October 12, 2010).
- Later outcomes cannot support the original investment underwriting.
- Numbers generated by the Financial Lab are **learner assumptions**, never claimed as Shopify’s realized 2011 results.
- Missing, retrospective and stale model sources create review warnings but do not block local draft exports.
- Investment committee questions are **pre-authored and deterministic**, not a live generative AI adversary or substantive professional marking.
- Slide copy is user-editable. Generated .pptx is intended for continued editing, not a final investment-committee-ready designed deliverable.

## Limitations / pending

- Automated XML ZIP checks are **not equivalent** to visually opening the PowerPoint and checking text layout in Microsoft PowerPoint, Keynote, or LibreOffice.
- No charts linked live to the worksheet, no PowerPoint designer freeform dragging or object-level handles, and no advanced templating/animation.
- No model-provided IC Q&A that dynamically reasons over an uploaded memo; scripted exercises only.
- No native mobile device Safari or iPad verification.
- Original video and four still references for the **Reference-Grounded Design Council have not been independently inspected and compared against the app screenshots in this phase**. Automated responsive QA is not aesthetic sign-off.
- Do not merge and do not deploy privately/publicly without explicit review of the relevant privacy and design requirements.
