## Mandate / 100-minute timebox

You are a PE Associate at an investment committee with a nominal **20% equity IRR** hurdle and 5-year hold. Founder asks **7.5× FY2025 EBITDA** for 100% of a fictional healthcare distributor. Debate **invest / conditionally bid / pass**, show a binding maximum EV and whether debt financing is survivable in year 1 and all five projected years. Sponsor can refuse rescue capital; credit approval is not guaranteed.

## Independent deliverables

1. Historical NWC and cash conversion hypothesis; at least 3 diligence requests distinguishing booked data from seller statements. **Do not create an unsupported 3-statement reconciliation using missing income statement detail.**
2. At asking price: exact seller share consideration, uses/sources and sponsor equity; purchase EV vs seller proceeds must be separate.
3. A five-year base/downside model with the specified cash waterfall, covenant gate, sponsor injections, final debt/revolver/cash and exit sponsor equity proceeds.
4. Sensitivity: 3 entry EV multiples × 3 exit multiples of your choosing (include both 6.5× and 5.5× among exits); solve maximum EV producing 20% base IRR **provided financing tests pass**.
5. A two-page independent IC memo, one explicit decision request, 3 falsifiable financing/collections gates, and a 5-minute oral defense of why a high modelled return may still mean **PASS**.
