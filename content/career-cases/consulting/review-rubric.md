# Independent case review rubric | SELF-internal, not a hiring-firm standard

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence for full points | Typical deductions |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Technical** | **30** | All E01/E02 totals reconcile; a correctly sequenced, zero-residual revenue and operating profit bridge; correctly labeled per-pack / thousand packs / million units; A/B/C, interactions, scenarios and payback formula checked; capex separated from profit | Any duplicated benefit, wrong signed price/cost effect, wrong discount cohort, mismatched time period, unverified workbook formulas |
| **Judgment** | **25** | Chooses feasible option or conditional hold; compares top alternative, accounts for seven slots and IDR 1,800m cash, evaluates opportunity costs, admits residual loss and proposes falsifiable gates | “Largest revenue wins,” recommends infeasible portfolio, treats hypothetical uptake as guaranteed |
| **Evidence** | **20** | Every fact/assumption/inference identified; provenance of all six synthetic exhibits; source pages never presented as SajiRasa numbers; matched validation requests for 3 pivotal hypotheses | Confuses sample interview with real client engagement, fabricates published data or causal proof |
| **Risk** | **15** | Full E06 scenario reforecast, explicit downside, food quality/service veto, contract elasticity, 90-day thresholds and owners | No operational go/no-go, no downside, payback stated as certain |
| **Communication** | **10** | Six answer-first headlines supported by exhibits, clean executive decision ask, 60-second synthesis with numbers and a credible caveat | Decorative dashboards, vague labels, slide headline unsupported by data |

### Proposed cutoffs
**Training-pass proposal:** 80/100; technical ≥24/30; evidence ≥14/20; all critical hard gates pass. These are internal design heuristics, **not** a validated psychometric screening standard or guarantee of job readiness. A real reviewer should independently score 2–3 candidate versions to calibrate consistency.

### Hard-stop failures
- Historical or segment amounts do not reconcile, or profit bridge residual is nonzero.
- The preferred option counts the same eligible packets as savings after those packs are removed by another option.
- Initiative spending ignores upfront cash/slots or conflates capex with earnings.
- Material source numbers or public firm affiliation are fabricated, including depicting a practice case as completed paid client work.
- The assessment reveals a sealed answer key before independent attempt; tests may contain expected deterministic assertions only outside browser-served material.
- No downside / no falsifiable kill gate / unsafe food-quality recommendation.
- Claim of independent professional QA without record of named independent reviewer.

### Automated vs manual verdict
The Node regression fixture verifies deterministic numeric invariants, manifest paths and structural requirements: **file-integrity and source-fixture QA only**. It cannot tell whether a candidate's PPT is compelling, whether supplier quotes are valid or whether any external firm endorses a conclusion.

Manual reviewer checklist (sign/date required):
- [ ] All 6 slides show action title, exact analytic source, true unit and caveat.
- [ ] Workbook calculations independently recomputed, including interaction and full-year vs 90-day distinction.
- [ ] Three alternative views scored; nonpreferred options get fair defense.
- [ ] Service/quality/contract-risk override evaluated.
- [ ] Candidate answered at least five Partner challenge questions including #1, #8, #9, #10, #18.
- [ ] No content slipped into `content/career-learning.json`, shared app/CI, or database from this worker.
