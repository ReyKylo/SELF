# Manager / Partner defense — skeptical questions, no canned answers
Deliver orally in 45–75 seconds each, **conclusion first**. A review partner should push back twice: “How do you know?” and “What would reverse your view?” Documentation must provide both a falsifier and the executive action.

## Evidence and modeling
1. Your profit waterfall reconciles, but **which observations prove causality?** Which are just attribution identities?
2. Would a pure cost-driven issue tree miss SKU/channel mix? What makes your partition non-overlapping?
3. If the 2026 figure is nine-month annualized, what seasonal pattern could invalidate your year-over-year comparison? How would you normalize it?
4. How do you avoid mistaking revenue price/mix effects for contribution-margin effects? Explain order dependence of price-volume-mix allocation.
5. The Core OTIF and return metrics deteriorate together. What matched cohorts or instrument can distinguish defective packs from routing delays or promotional channel changes?
6. Why can't you treat “reachable” accounts from E05 as evidence of conversion and actual orders?

## Economics and operating constraints
7. How does B's promo affect **existing** Premium packs? Demonstrate with a unit waterfall, not a percentage assertion.
8. Why is C's price increase calculated on survivors, not all original B2B Core packs? What if price elasticity is nonlinear?
9. Where exactly does overlap enter A+C and A+B? If C's lost packs are noneligible suppliers, what changes?
10. Distinguish capex, one-time execution expenses, operating profit, recurring run-rate, and true cash payback. Where would working capital enter if disclosed?
11. How would your preferred portfolio change if the CEO could authorize only **five** slots? Which single option survives, and why?
12. If your highest base-upside option cannot be executed in seven slots, should you rank it at all? How do you communicate a feasible set?
13. Does a positive downside still justify rollout if product recall risk becomes non-financially unacceptable? Who owns a veto?
14. Can a limited pilot create supplier lock-in before failure criteria are measurable? Which contract clauses preserve reversibility?

## Executive judgment and communication
15. Why is recovering 2025 profitability **not guaranteed** even if annualized initiative assumptions hold?
16. The head of sales says Core cancellations are due to service delays, not price. What new evidence would overturn your priority?
17. If supplier negotiation savings hold at only 60% eligibility, how would you alter the pilot scale, cash authorization and payback disclosure?
18. What exactly is the CEO authorizing on Day 0? Name a number, cap, and owner. Is that a rollout or diligence spend?
19. Show the single chart that most changes your recommendation and explain why other charts belong in the appendix.
20. State two risks not quantified in your workbook and how you would prevent false confidence.

## Review technique
For each answer, assessor records: **claim; numerical or source evidence; assumption; disconfirming test; decision implication**. “It depends” alone is incomplete. Alternative recommendations are acceptable if their model, gates and tradeoff defense are coherent.
