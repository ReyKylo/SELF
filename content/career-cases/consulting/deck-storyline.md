# Six-slide CEO deck specification — no decorative slides
**Assignment, not a completed client deck.** Exactly 6 content slides, one key question / decision per slide. Visible title = the conclusion the evidence can support, not a topic name. Numbers in title must tie to exhibit and model tab; cite synthetic E## in footer. Do not fabricate a McKinsey, Bain or BCG template or stamp any consulting firm logo.

| # | CEO question and conclusion-oriented **action-title template** | Analytical exhibit, one per slide | Required implication and footnote |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Executive recommendation | “Authorize **[initiative/pair] conditionally**; commit **[upfront IDR m]** only after **[validated gate]**” | 3-row decision summary: candidate portfolio / annual recurring uplift / up-front cash / feasibility | State exactly what CEO approves now and what remains conditional. Footnote steady-state rather than 90-day benefit. |
| 2 — Profitability problem | “A **[IDR m]** profit drop far outpaces **[x%]** revenue change” | 2024–26 revenue / variable / fixed / profit mini-P&L with annualized 2026 clearly patterned or labeled | Quantify cash-fixed vs contribution; forbid labeling 2026 actual. |
| 3 — Root-cause evidence | “**[top driver]** explains **[contribution]** of the decline, but causal mechanism remains **[status]**” | Four-driver waterfall: 2025 profit + quantity + price + per-unit cost + fixed = 2026 run-rate profit; side note Core service/returns | Explicitly show evidence vs testable hypothesis; do not use an unverified causal verb. |
| 4 — Quantified alternatives | “**[option/pair]** wins under cash/slots after **[interaction]** is removed” | Standalone and feasible pair decision matrix with recurring profit, execution cost, capex, payback, overlap, 7-slot & 1,800 cash hard limits | No benefit double-count; distinguish run-rate from first-year; annotate infeasible alternatives rather than omitting. |
| 5 — 90-day execution | “Pilot **[actions]** first to verify **[two critical assumptions]** before capital release” | 0–15 / 16–45 / 46–70 / 71–90 timeline with owner, observable KPI, baseline and threshold | Assign CFO, COO, Quality, Sales and CEO; call pilot commitment and rollout commitment separately. |
| 6 — Risks and decision gates | “Scale only if **[conditions]** hold; otherwise preserve cash and reverse **[exposure]**” | Base/downside/upside recurring benefit, relative service and churn risk, trigger → owner → stop / revise | State independent negative evidence and legal/quality caveats; discuss downside even if still positive. |

### Slide construction exercise — table to decision
1. **Source table**: E02 has 8 rows. Calculate period-aligned SKU×channel contribution; identify where change occurred.
2. **Chart**: waterfall **only if** its four effects sum from 2025 profit exactly to 2026 estimated profit. Show a visible reconciliation check. A bar chart of unnormalized volume and money is forbidden.
3. **Headline**: write the conclusion after math, with time period, unit and uncertainty, e.g. “Unit cost inflation is a stronger *arithmetic* driver than volume” only if validated by the bridge.
4. **Caveat**: a distinct note says “cause not yet verified,” with a specific empirical next test.
5. **Action**: request approval for one next decision, not “monitor the market.”
6. **Partner red pen**: highlight any title unsupported by the exhibit and rewrite it before final submission.

### Layout mechanics
Use wide horizontal alignment, one main analytical display, consistent IDR m units, 12-point-or-larger readable footnotes in standard 16:9 deck, neutral colors with a **single highlight only** for the decision; green/red should mean performance or gate and not merely decoration. No generic AI backgrounds, icons or faux corporate branding. Avoid 12 tiny panels. Print/export to PDF only as a shareable view, while keeping the source editable.

### Executive storytelling and verbal delivery
Prepare a **60-second answer-first talk track**: decision, quantified economic rationale, major caveat, quantified downside, what to approve today. A partner should be able to locate every number in the workbook and every risk in a named gate.

### Model-to-deck traceability checklist
Slide 1 ↔ Option Economics + Constraints; Slide 2 ↔ E01 + Segment P&L; Slide 3 ↔ Profit Bridge + E03; Slide 4 ↔ E04 + E06; Slide 5 ↔ Evidence Log + 90-day tracker; Slide 6 ↔ Scenarios + risks and stop criteria. No narrative statement without a link to input or explicit judgment.
