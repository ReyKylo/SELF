This taskbook is a **SELF internal scoring proposal**. It is not a CFA or PE employer rubric. No automatic high score implies portfolio readiness; independent review is required.

### A. Historical and earnings-quality lab

Recompute each year rather than trusting Exhibit 01: revenue/COGS/Opex/EBITDA; EBITDA−depreciation−interest−tax; NWC, ΔNWC, CFO, capex, ending debt, ending cash and balance sheet equation. For 2023 cash/debt/NWC bridges, 2022 *opening* assumptions are cash **12**, debt **39.14**, NWC **40** (all simulated). Build 2023–25 *year-end proxy* DSO/DIO/DPO/CCC and NWC/revenue; disclose proxy limitations. Evaluate the common EBITDA add-back proposal “overdue collections will normalize” and explain why it's not automatically an EBITDA adjustment.

**Diligence exhibits:** quantify trade AR more than 90 days old and inventory more than 180 days old. Explain the difference between *age at risk* and booked impairment. Demand two independent collection proofs and two SKU-level follow-ups.

### B. Terms and funding

At seller ask and a **price you select**, write Sources & Uses with seller proceeds, old debt payoff, fee, usable cash, sponsor equity and closing cash floor. Explain the EV vs equity difference in one sentence to a non-finance CEO. Compare entry EV/EBITDA, lender debt/EBITDA and sponsor cash exposure (they are not interchangeable).

### C. Forecast, debt and cash waterfall

Reconstruct ten scenario-year forecasts directly from Exhibits 03–05 (5 base + 5 downside). Show these columns, not just summary returns: beginning debt/cash/revolver; sales, EBITDA, depreciation, interest, tax, NWC, ΔNWC, capex, CFADS, mandatory amort, revolver draws/repayments, rescue equity, sweep, ending term/revolver/cash, net leverage, DSCR and covenant PASS/FAIL. If any fixed debt commitment is unavailable in real diligence, label it an unmet condition precedent.

**Stress logic:** Project DOWNSIDE without unfinanced negative cash; first use 25 revolver availability; when exhausted, label every sponsor cash injection explicitly. If lender covenant fails despite rescue equity, record **not compliant / waiver needed**, rather than asserting the funding keeps the debt legally current.

### D. Pricing and returns

No interim shareholder distributions. At 2030 exit, compute EBITDA×exit multiple − closing term debt − closing revolver + cash. Model at least entry EV **260, 273, 285** and exit **6×, 7×, 8×**, then calculate sponsor MOIC and annual IRR in BASE (with no recap); crosscheck Exhibit 06 but do not copy values directly. Compute max entry EV for 20% sponsor IRR if exit is 7×, then if exit compresses to 6×. Expose assumption that exit debt and cash are unaffected by purchase EV because debt and operations are held fixed. For DOWNSIDE, use time-stamped emergency sponsor capital calls in IRR equation, not a simple average.

### E. Two-page investment committee memo specification

- **Page 1:** 2-sentence verdict; requested EV, suggested price cap and hurdle; 3-year growth versus cash reconciliation; quality of earnings concerns; Sources & Uses; what operating fixes must happen.
- **Page 2:** base/downside lender and returns table, maximum-bid boundary, 3 priced alternatives (including pass), 3 falsifiable diligence/credit gates with deadlines, deal-breaking conditions, an explicit IC approval/decline ask.
- Add footnotes linking each quantified claim to a *simulated exhibit*, mark seller statements as simulated and your probability judgements as analyst inference. No claim of independently verified market comp multiple.

### F. Skeptical IC partner — live questions

1. “EBITDA naik hampir dua kali, kenapa masih kekurangan uang buat bayar bank? Show me 2025 cash bridge and 2026 DSCR.”
2. “Why is 7× defensible when a 6× exit compresses our return? What is the maximum price for 20% return under 6×?”
3. “Your downside needs new equity *and* violates DSCR. Why do you call this financeable? What covenant, borrowing-base or purchase condition must change?”
4. “Sixteen of AR is >90 days and 17 inventory is >180 days — what's the evidence that these are collectible/salable and not a hidden loss?”
5. “If sponsor has to inject cash in year 2, what happens to the denominator and IRR? Which assumption is most brittle?”

### Submission audit

Before giving to reviewer, record version/date, original dataset SHA or commit, exact forecast inputs, a separate XLSX/ODS file created by learner if applicable, saved outputs, at least 3 resolved model errors and a **revision log** (claim changed → evidence → impact). Do not upload confidential employer data. Confirm you can explain every formula without the coach page.
