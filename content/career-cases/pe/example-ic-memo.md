**Simulated model mechanics (Exhibits 03–06):** 11% base term interest on beginning balances; annual mandatory repayment 7; revolver 25; 50% excess cash sweep; no interim dividends. **Multiple downside:** If the 2030 exit multiple compresses to **6×** instead of 7× without changing company performance, purchase EV at which a 20% 5-year IRR is exactly met falls to **~272.57**, assuming unchanged debt, closing fees and cash floor. The base 7× ceiling **~300.54** is *mechanical*, not an executable maximum bid: it ignores downside insolvency risk, missing financing commitments and qualitative loss severity. Negotiation ceiling **260** imposes an additional cushion to multiple compression; no 6× exit guarantee exists.

**Operating downside cannot be represented by a reassuring IRR alone.** With 2026 EBITDA **~36.25**, 13% term interest and elevated NWC, FY2026 **DSCR ~0.425×** (vs 1.00× required), drawing **~13.749** revolver to maintain cash at 12. With *assumed lender tolerance despite contractual breaches*, FY2030 exit at 7× yields **~223.273** equity proceeds; dated sponsor equity cash flows at EV 273 yield **~5.57% IRR**, not an investable approved plan. We would **not** count an unapproved waiver as liquidity availability.

1. **Cash quality (30 days):** third-party confirm invoice-level receipts/offsets on at least **16 aged AR**, reconcile 2025 cash flow to bank statements, and substantiate **17** aged inventory via SKU-level sale/expiry/return history.
