## Product, business and history

ArusOps (fictional, founded January 2024 in Jakarta) sells approval routing, supplier purchase orders, invoice matching and outlet-level inventory reconciliation for multi-outlet food/retail operators. A typical outlet manager sends photos of invoices through chat; finance manually matches them to the purchase order. Software promises less spreadsheet rework and traceable approvals. Founder narrative: product onboarding in two weeks and measurable finance-team savings. **Neither claim has been independently validated.**

Pricing (**SIMULATED**): SMB US$180/month per company and MID US$1,100/month per company, not per outlet; usage expansions increase same-account recurring invoices by 1.2% and 5.5% per cohort age-month respectively in the constructed exercise. No annual discounts or taxes are modeled. Installation fees are one-time and **not ARR**.

Timeline: Jan 2024 prototype; Oct 2025 first tracked production cohort; Apr 2026 a MID-focused sales team begins aggressive expansion; by Sep 2026 the company reports 864 paid live customers and live ARR of US$3,542,040. Earlier launch customers and debt are **assumed absent** from this simplified universe; sales funnel conversion and real gross margin invoices are not provided.

## Diligence plan (evidence that changes a decision)

1. **Revenue ledger:** sample 30 invoices per segment, bank cash receipts, signed contracts, start dates, cancellation/reinstatement events; reconcile US$1.2m booked-but-not-live. Gate: at least 95% recurring MRR match in transaction sample with no systemic setup-fee contamination (threshold is case policy).
2. **Retention causality:** sample 20 churned SMBs + 15 retained MID customers independently, stratified by cohort and channel. Gate: explain >75% of churned MRR by coded reasons and produce 90-day SMB retention experiment with measured baseline; absence of evidence blocks investment.
3. **Sales efficiency & liquidity:** payroll-to-CAC bridge, channel paid invoices, implementation labor, receivables aging and six-month weekly cash plan; downside stress US$420k/month forward net burn. Gate: verified >6 months runway at closing (including funded cash and binding commitments), no unfunded cash gap before close.
4. **Contractual structure:** counsel-redline preference stack, option-pool timing, pro-rata rights, anti-dilution, corporate authority, cap-table ledger; independently recalc fund FD percentage.
5. **Market proof:** 25 qualified prospects and 10 lost deals, independently sourced; count addressable multi-outlet companies and confirm realistic ACV, purchase authority, switching cost and integration economics.

## Decision alternatives

- **INVEST:** only with an actual price, term, ownership, 5x route, high-confidence data and release gates.
- **PASS:** product may be excellent, but share price and forward dilution may leave inadequate venture return.
- **REQUEST FURTHER DILIGENCE:** make it time-bound, identify blockers, owners and explicit thresholds; this is **not** unconditional approval.
