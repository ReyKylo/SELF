# Investment committee artifact and skeptic defense (SIMULATED)

**Deliverable specification — not a completed investment recommendation.**

## Two-page IC memo

**Page 1 — decision:** Headline should name decision (INVEST / PASS / REQUEST FURTHER DILIGENCE), maximum entry valuation and initial check; 2-sentence business; fund fit/ownership/hurdle; leading upside with one **verified-in-simulation** growth signal; strongest counter-evidence from cohort retention, burn and dilution. One compact table: **growth | retention | CAC | runway | ownership** with date, unit, numerator/denominator and source cell. Conclude exactly what approval is being requested **today** and what is NOT approved.

**Page 2 — underwriting:** TAM/SAM/SOM with conversion assumptions and status **SIMULATED**; competitor substitute/win-loss gaps; current vs raised/post-diluted cap table; four valuation and exit scenarios with preferred waterfall; 3 risk gates (metric, who supplies primary proof, by when, invest-if/pass-if). Alternatives including passing and structured/staged diligence. Appendix (not page count) may contain cash/retention and model links.

**No fabricated data room evidence, founder credentials, real customer references or professional endorsement.** If evidence is simulated, label it in table title/footnote, not only at front.

## Partner challenge questions (defend without tutor)

1. Why call this strong growth if same-base NRR is below 100%? Quantify how much of September growth is entirely new logos.
2. Would you rather have 1,000 shrinking small customers or 100 expanding larger customers? Why is answer conditional on concentration and churn causes?
3. Is 15% option pool measured post-money, unallocated or including already granted options? Who absorbs top-up and why?
4. You're praising eight-month CAC payback; what if 40% of clients churn before breakeven and full implementation staff was excluded?
5. Which part of US$1.2m signed backlog turns into revenue, by exactly what dates? What does a bank statement establish versus MRR?
6. If the 2028 round is 15m at only 25m pre instead of 45m, what is your lead stake, and what secondary financing might be required?
7. Does US$300m exit really fund a 5x, or did you forget dilution, liquidation preferences and cash-on-cash losses?
8. Why does the modeled US$35m ARR plan exceed the initial five-year US$18m SOM? What new evidence would reconcile the two?
9. Why could a great product produce a bad investment at US$24m pre? What valuation gives you enough expected MOIC under your probability mix?
10. Under exactly what 30-day diligence result would you reverse your preferred recommendation?
11. In the authentic 2009 Twilio memo, why was a seed extension rational before committing to the large Series A? Do not invoke later outcomes.
12. In Toast's original memo, why might 'eight-month payback on live ARR' differ from actual cash payback? How would that apply to ArusOps?
